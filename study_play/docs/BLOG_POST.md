# DSA Study Tracker

A short note on why I built a local-first tracker for my 12-week interview prep — and how you can try it.

Interview prep usually fails from missing rhythm, not missing problems. I already had a study plan and drills in this repo; I needed one page that turns the plan into daily action and keeps a record of every solve.

So I built **DSA Study Tracker**: daily checklists, a problem log with lessons and revisit dates, weekly heat-map stats, and an optional Gemini analyzer — all in the browser, no backend.

**Try it:** open [`study_tracker.html`](./study_tracker.html) or see the [README](./README.md) for setup.  
**Repo:** [github.com/rakshyak-98/dsa-problem](https://github.com/rakshyak-98/dsa-problem)

Consistency beats intensity. Log one problem, write one lesson.
